# Security Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

This policy covers security vulnerabilities in the DeepSeek Harness for Android source code and application. It does not authorize testing of DeepSeek, model providers, plugin authors' systems, Android services, or other third-party infrastructure.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report suspected vulnerabilities to [security@comicine.com](mailto:security@comicine.com). Include, where possible:

- A clear description of the affected component.
- Steps to reproduce the issue.
- The security impact and relevant technical evidence.
- A suggested fix, if available.

Do not include API keys, passwords, private conversations, or other unnecessary personal or sensitive information in a report. If sensitive information is exposed during research, stop testing and report it promptly.

## Scope and research guidelines

Only this application's code and systems that ComiCine owns and has explicitly authorized are in scope. Association with ComiCine does not make a model provider, plugin, dependency, external service, or connected system part of the authorized scope. Request written authorization before testing anything whose scope is unclear.

Researchers are asked to act in good faith, use the minimum testing needed to demonstrate an issue, avoid accessing or changing other users' data, and avoid service disruption, denial of service, phishing, social engineering, physical attacks, or destructive testing. Coordinating disclosure before publishing an unresolved vulnerability helps reduce risk, but this policy does not impose a specific response or remediation deadline.

This policy does not itself grant authorization, create a legal safe harbor, promise immunity from legal action, or operate a bug bounty program. Researchers remain responsible for obtaining authorization and complying with applicable law and service terms. A report does not authorize further access or testing.

## Acknowledgments and compensation

ComiCine may choose whether to publish an acknowledgment for a verified report. A researcher's name or identifying information will not be published without consent. Recognition does not imply payment; no monetary compensation or other reward is promised.

## Contact

Email [security@comicine.com](mailto:security@comicine.com) to report a vulnerability or clarify the scope of authorized testing.
